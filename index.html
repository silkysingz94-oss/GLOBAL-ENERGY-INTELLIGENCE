Data methodology

Country and year values are taken from the latest available global dataset records. Different indicators may have different latest years.

Electricity access is a population coverage indicator, not a measure of reliability, hours of supply, affordability, or grid connection quality.

Electricity generation and consumption are not interchangeable. National generation may include different accounting scopes, cross-border electricity trade and losses.

OpenStreetMap infrastructure completeness varies considerably by country. Absence of a mapped power feature does not prove absence of infrastructure.

Household data are optional user-provided JSON. No household counts are invented.
